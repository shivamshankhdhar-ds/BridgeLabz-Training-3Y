What Is a Tooltip?

A tooltip is a small information box that appears when a user hovers over or focuses on an element.

How It Works

  • position: relative; anchors the tooltip to its parent.

  • opacity: controls tooltip visibility.
  • transition: creates a smooth fade-in effect.
